Questions

What type of data can I upload?

Any Excel or CSV table with a header row: sales, expenses, students, HR, inventory, surveys and more. Mirai finds the numbers, categories and dates on its own. More integrations will come later.

Do I need SQL knowledge?

No. Mirai is designed so business users can ask questions in natural language.

Can Mirai create dashboards automatically?

Yes. Try the demo above: upload a file and Mirai builds a dashboard from it.

Can I ask questions about my data?

Yes. Mirai uses AI to answer free-form questions, and it calculates every number from your file instead of guessing. The full product with accounts and saved history is being built.

Can Mirai forecast sales?

Forecasting based on your history is on the roadmap.

Is my data secure?

In the demo, your file is read in your browser and not uploaded. Security details for the full product will be published before launch.

Can multiple employees use one account?

Yes, on the Business plan.
